HEALTHY LIFE

A simple guide to building healthy habits for everyday life.

WHO THIS IS FOR

Anyone who wants to improve their health and live a healthier lifestyle.

THE IDEA IN ONE LINE

Small healthy habits can make a big difference in your daily life.

BEFORE YOU START

1. Set your goals. Decide what healthy habits you want to improve.
2. Make a schedule. Plan time for exercise, meals, and rest.
3. Eat healthy food. Choose fruits, vegetables, and balanced meals.
4. Drink enough water. Stay hydrated throughout the day.

DAILY HEALTHY HABITS

5. Exercise regularly. Try walking, jogging, or cycling.
6. Get enough sleep. Aim for 7 to 9 hours each night if you are an adult.
7. Eat breakfast. Choose a nutritious breakfast when it fits your routine.

TAKING CARE OF YOUR BODY

8. Keep yourself clean. Practise good personal hygiene.
9. Reduce junk food. Limit foods high in sugar, salt, and unhealthy fats.
10. Take short breaks. Rest your body and mind during busy days.

WHEN YOU FEEL TIRED OR STRESSED

11. Take a rest. Give your body time to recover.
12. Relax your mind. Try listening to music or doing relaxing activities.
13. Spend time outdoors. Enjoy fresh air and nature.
14. Talk to someone. Share your feelings with family or friends.
15. Seek help when needed. Consult a healthcare professional if you have ongoing health concerns.

CALL TO ACTION

Start your healthy lifestyle journey today.
